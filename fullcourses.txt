DATA PROTECTION & PRIVACY COURSES (DP SERIES) {#dp-series}
DP-101: Introduction to Data Protection Fundamentals
Duration: 3 Days Comprehensive introduction to global data protection principles, covering basic concepts of personal data, processing activities, and fundamental rights. Students learn the evolution of privacy laws from early legislation to modern comprehensive frameworks like GDPR and NDPA.
DP-102: Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA) Compliance
Duration: 5 Days Deep dive into Nigeria's Data Protection Act 2023, covering legal requirements, enforcement mechanisms, and practical compliance strategies. Includes hands-on workshops on data mapping, privacy notices, and consent mechanisms specific to Nigerian regulatory environment.
DP-103: GDPR Implementation and Compliance
Duration: 4 Days Comprehensive coverage of European Union's General Data Protection Regulation, focusing on extraterritorial application, lawful basis for processing, and data subject rights. Practical sessions on GDPR compliance for organizations with EU operations or data subjects.
DP-104: Cross-Border Data Transfer Regulations
Duration: 3 Days Advanced course on international data transfer mechanisms, adequacy decisions, Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs), and Binding Corporate Rules (BCRs). Critical for multinational organizations and data processors serving global clients.
DP-105: Data Subject Rights Management
Duration: 2 Days Focused training on managing data subject access requests, right to rectification, erasure, portability, and objection. Includes practical workshops on response procedures, timeframes, and exemptions under various jurisdictions.
DP-201: Privacy Impact Assessment (PIA) Methodology
Duration: 4 Days Advanced course on conducting comprehensive Privacy Impact Assessments, risk identification, mitigation strategies, and regulatory consultation processes. Hands-on experience with PIA templates and real-world case studies.
DP-202: Privacy by Design and Default Implementation
Duration: 3 Days Technical and procedural approaches to embedding privacy into systems and processes from inception. Covers proactive measures, full functionality, end-to-end security, and user-centric design principles.
DP-203: Data Breach Response and Incident Management
Duration: 3 Days Comprehensive breach response procedures, notification requirements, documentation protocols, and communication strategies. Includes simulation exercises and regulatory reporting requirements across multiple jurisdictions.
DP-204: International Privacy Laws Comparative Analysis
Duration: 5 Days Comparative study of major privacy frameworks including GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, PIPEDA, Lei Geral de Proteção de Dados (LGPD), and emerging African privacy laws. Essential for global privacy professionals.
DP-301: Advanced Privacy Engineering
Duration: 5 Days Technical implementation of privacy controls, differential privacy, homomorphic encryption, secure multi-party computation, and privacy-preserving analytics. Designed for technical professionals and privacy engineers.
DP-302: Privacy in Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning
Duration: 4 Days Specialized course on privacy challenges in AI/ML systems, algorithmic accountability, fairness, transparency, and automated decision-making compliance. Covers model privacy, data minimization in ML, and explainable AI requirements.
DP-303: Healthcare and Financial Services Privacy
Duration: 4 Days Industry-specific privacy requirements for healthcare (HIPAA, medical device regulations) and financial services (PCI DSS, banking privacy regulations). Includes sector-specific risk assessments and compliance frameworks.

CYBERSECURITY FUNDAMENTALS (CS SERIES) {#cs-series}
CS-101: Cybersecurity Fundamentals and Awareness
Duration: 2 Days Foundation course covering basic cybersecurity concepts, threat landscape, risk assessment, and security awareness. Designed for all organizational roles to build security-conscious culture and basic threat recognition skills.
CS-102: Network Security Essentials
Duration: 4 Days Comprehensive coverage of network security principles, firewalls, intrusion detection systems, VPNs, and network monitoring. Hands-on labs with network security tools and configuration exercises.
CS-103: Information Security Risk Management
Duration: 3 Days Systematic approach to identifying, assessing, and managing information security risks. Covers risk frameworks (ISO 27005, NIST), quantitative and qualitative risk analysis, and risk treatment strategies.
CS-104: Security Policies and Procedures Development
Duration: 3 Days Creating comprehensive security policies, procedures, and guidelines aligned with business objectives and regulatory requirements. Includes policy lifecycle management and employee awareness programs.
CS-105: Incident Response Fundamentals
Duration: 3 Days Basic incident response procedures, detection, containment, eradication, and recovery processes. Includes hands-on incident simulation exercises and documentation requirements.
CS-201: Vulnerability Assessment and Management
Duration: 4 Days Systematic approach to vulnerability identification, assessment, prioritization, and remediation. Hands-on experience with vulnerability scanning tools and patch management strategies.
CS-202: Security Awareness Training and Culture Development
Duration: 2 Days Designing and implementing effective security awareness programs, measuring effectiveness, and building security-conscious organizational culture. Includes phishing simulation and security behavior modification techniques.
CS-203: Business Continuity and Disaster Recovery
Duration: 4 Days Comprehensive business continuity planning, disaster recovery strategies, backup systems, and crisis management. Includes business impact analysis and recovery time/point objectives setting.

ADVANCED CYBERSECURITY (AC SERIES) {#ac-series}
AC-101: Advanced Threat Detection and Analysis
Duration: 5 Days Advanced threat hunting techniques, behavioral analysis, advanced persistent threat (APT) detection, and threat intelligence utilization. Hands-on experience with SIEM tools and threat analysis platforms.
AC-102: Penetration Testing Methodology
Duration: 10 Days Comprehensive penetration testing training covering reconnaissance, scanning, exploitation, post-exploitation, and reporting. Extensive hands-on labs in controlled environments with various attack vectors.
AC-103: Digital Forensics and Investigation
Duration: 7 Days Computer forensics methodology, evidence collection, chain of custody, forensic tools, and legal considerations. Practical exercises in forensic investigation using industry-standard tools and techniques.

AC-105: Malware Analysis and Reverse Engineering
Duration: 8 Days Static and dynamic malware analysis techniques, reverse engineering methodologies, and malware family identification. Advanced course requiring technical background in programming and system internals.
AC-201: Cloud Security Architecture
Duration: 5 Days Comprehensive cloud security across AWS, Azure, and Google Cloud platforms. Covers shared responsibility models, cloud security tools, configuration management, and compliance in cloud environments.
AC-202: Mobile Device Security and Management
Duration: 3 Days Mobile security frameworks, mobile device management (MDM), application security, and BYOD policies. Covers iOS and Android security features and enterprise mobility management.
AC-203: Industrial Control Systems (ICS) Security
Duration: 4 Days SCADA and ICS security, operational technology (OT) environments, industrial protocols, and critical infrastructure protection. Specialized course for industrial and utility sector professionals.
AC-301: Advanced Cryptography and Key Management

AC-302: Security Operations Center (SOC) Management
Duration: 4 Days SOC design, implementation, and management. Covers SOC processes, tool integration, analyst training, and performance metrics. Essential for SOC managers and senior security analysts.
